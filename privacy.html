life-ops privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-09

life-ops is a personal tool used by one person, its developer (Andrew Barkett), on his own Google account. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit OAuth consent, life-ops can read Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, and Google Drive, Docs and Sheets files in the owner's account. If the owner also grants write access, it can save Gmail drafts (it never sends email) and create calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner organize his own tasks, bills, home maintenance and writing. It is processed on the owner's own computers and in AI assistant sessions he controls. It is not sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising. It is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's devices and in his private configuration. Summaries the owner chooses to keep are stored in his private records. Raw message contents are not retained beyond the task in progress.

Google API Services User Data Policy

life-ops's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
